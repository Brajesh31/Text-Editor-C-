# Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

This project is maintained as a place to learn, build, review, and discuss software. Participation should be constructive and respectful, regardless of experience level, background, identity, or preferred tools. Beginners should be able to ask questions without being mocked, and experienced contributors should be able to disagree without personal attacks.

## Expected behavior

Community members are expected to:
- Be respectful, patient, and considerate in discussions and reviews.
- Critique code, decisions, and technical proposals without insulting the people involved.
- Explain technical feedback with evidence, examples, or reproducible observations.
- Accept that contributors have different levels of experience and different environments.
- Give credit for useful contributions and acknowledge corrections.
- Keep discussions focused on the project when using repository issues and pull requests.
- Respect privacy and obtain permission before sharing another person's personal information.
- Follow GitHub's terms and applicable laws.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:
- Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, or targeted abuse.
- Personal attacks, deliberate humiliation, or repeated hostile comments.
- Sexualized language or unwanted sexual attention.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Trolling, sustained disruption, or deliberately derailing technical discussions.
- Spam, impersonation, or malicious links.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces, including repository issues, pull requests, code reviews, discussions, and project-related communication where a person is representing or participating in this project.

## Reporting a concern

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Do not post sensitive details in a public issue. Include relevant links, dates, and a concise description when it is safe to do so.

Reports will be reviewed as promptly as practical. The maintainer may ask for clarification, preserve relevant evidence, or take action to protect participants.

## Maintainer responsibilities and enforcement

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying expected standards and responding to reported behavior. Depending on severity and context, actions may include:
1. A private or public reminder of the expected standard.
2. A warning and request to stop the behavior.
3. Temporary restrictions on participation.
4. Removal of comments or other content where platform controls allow it.
5. A longer-term or permanent restriction from project spaces.

The response will consider the impact, pattern, severity, and context of the behavior. Repeated or severe conduct may result in immediate restriction.

## Fairness and confidentiality

Reports will be handled with discretion. Information will be shared only when reasonably necessary to review or address the concern. The maintainer will avoid public speculation about reports and will not tolerate retaliation against good-faith reporters.

## A note on disagreement

Technical disagreement is welcome. It is acceptable to challenge an implementation, ask for evidence, request benchmarks, or reject a proposal. It is not acceptable to attack a contributor. Strong technical standards and respectful communication are compatible.

## Acknowledgment

By participating in this repository, you agree to follow this Code of Conduct and to help maintain a constructive project environment.
